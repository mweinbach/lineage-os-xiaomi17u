## Recreating a retired expansion

Historical replay that expects one of these image paths must rematerialize it.
Use that row's retained ZIP and exact `member` from the removal receipt, extract
only that member to a new staging directory, and verify the recorded SHA256 and
size before restoring its former path. For the transfer Super, copy the recorded
`keeper` and verify the same values. Do not overwrite a successor output.
Historical receipts describe the original run; re-extraction does not reproduce
original inode or timestamp evidence. Use a fresh phase/output directory for a
new verification or build rather than presenting rematerialization as that run.
