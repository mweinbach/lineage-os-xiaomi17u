# V25 log audit, September 12, 2026

**Two confirmed ROM defects have concrete fix paths: disabled VoLTE/video-call
capability flags, and the recurring QMI helper failure.** IWLAN also has an
unconfigured framework binding, but enabling Wi-Fi calling requires additional
carrier and provider validation. This audit collected logs and queried services
read-only; no phone setting, process, policy or image was changed.

The phone reports v25 (`nezha.b2c99443fe9e90a4a7954eac`), slot A, boot completed,
SELinux Enforcing and ADB shell UID 2000. Private captures and resource dumps are
under `reports/log-audit-20260912/`. The accompanying
[measurement record](../research/log-audit-20260912.json) pins the supporting
files. Log buffers retain different intervals; message counts are observations
within this capture, not lifetime totals. Earlier installation statements do not
establish the current v25 feature inventory.

The [implementation follow-up](telephony-fixes-20260912.md) records the later
source fixes, component results and deeper display/Binder investigation.

## Fix order

| Priority | Finding and evidence | Next implementation and validation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **VoLTE and carrier video calling are disabled by the ROM's device capabilities.** Both `config_device_volte_available` and `config_device_vt_available` resolve to `false` in the live phone application's resource context. The retained factory framework sets both to `true`. Fi's carrier configuration enables VoLTE; `ImsManager` reports valid GBA, provisioned VoLTE and the user's enhanced-4G setting enabled, but `isVolteEnabledByPlatform=false`. IMS remains unregistered. | Add the two factory-backed device capability booleans to the guarded framework overlay associated with IMS. Preserve carrier provisioning checks. Verify compiled resource values, then after an authorized installation verify registration, an ordinary call and SMS. Removing this proven platform blocker does not guarantee carrier registration or explain every modem error. |
| 2 | **`vendor.qmipriod` exits every five seconds.** There are 389 distinct status-255 exits between 19:50:55 and 20:23:19, and 497 unique audit records denying directory search on its own `vendor_qmipriod_data_file`. This reproduces the [previous diagnosis](tier1-ims-dim-20260909.md). | Complete the bounded vendor-policy derivation for access to the helper's own directory, retaining neverallow and image validation. Confirm whether further permissions are needed from a measured run. The existing alternative of disabling its persisted start property is a separate phone modification, not performed here. |
| 3 | **The IWLAN framework binding is unconfigured.** The installed `vendor.qti.iwlan` APK exports the network, data and qualified-network services, but all three package selectors resolve empty in the phone application's resource context, carrier overrides are empty, and ActivityManager reports no running IWLAN service. The log contains 78 `NRM-I-0: service not connected. Domain = PS` errors. | Prepare a guarded binding to the installed provider and validate its Java/native/policy dependencies before delivery. Confirm successful binding and WLAN network registration before calling it a Wi-Fi calling fix. Fi currently sets `carrier_wfc_ims_available_bool=false`, and the user WFC setting is false; preserve those gates. |

The retained factory base framework also has empty IWLAN selectors and
`config_device_wfc_ims_available=false`; the bounded overlay comparison found no
override for these keys. Consequently the IWLAN finding is a missing integration
in the current framework, **not evidence that copying one stock flag will enable
Fi Wi-Fi calling**. The stock-backed capability restoration in priority 1 covers
VoLTE and carrier video calling only.

The inspected `ImsManager.isVolteEnabledByPlatform()` evaluates the device
resource, the carrier flag and GBA validity together. The local source is AOSP
`frameworks/opt/net/ims` commit `336af62cf98cdf44247c78b9645b5a6535837624`, verified
against the peeled upstream `android-16.0.0_r4` tag. The inspected network binding
logic is in `frameworks/opt/telephony` commit
`bd2be122661748dae9251368418fc5efa50f5f08`; the current Evolution `bka` remote head
was recorded as `43d9cbbf116d28b870097fd9dd3397d94e99ee73`. These source trees are
clean; neither was updated. `NRM-I` denotes the WLAN transport, and its binding
method returns without binding when the package is empty. AOSP documents the
same selector roles in its
[telephony resources](https://android.googlesource.com/platform/frameworks/base/+/b68a7b78226be7fe879c79c5b43d071f26262b92/core/res/res/values/config_telephony.xml).

## Why there are thousands of errors

The QMI helper failures repeatedly trigger
`flags_health_check UPDATABLE_CRASHING`. Matching the init launch PID to the libc
messages attributes **4,037 of the 4,491 long-property-name errors** to twelve
of those health-check processes. The remaining 454 messages are unattributed in
this capture. Fixing the helper loop should remove that repeated trigger; it is
not necessary to treat every property name as a separate ROM defect. Battery or
performance improvement has not been measured.

## Further investigations, without a proven fix yet

- **Cellular service drops:** the service history records approximately
  16–19-second outages around 19:52:54, 20:04:54 and 20:16:54, followed by LTE
  recovery. IMS disconnection records carry vendor extra codes 4001 and 4002;
  their meanings have not been established here. First remove the proven VoLTE
  capability blocker, then correlate modem registration and data-call reasons
  if the drops remain. These logs do not establish that IWLAN or QMI failures
  cause the cellular outages. No call, SMS or cellular-download test occurred.
- **Display vendor command:** six requests, ten seconds apart, fail with
  `MiDisplayDM: setFeature, invalid parameter` followed by
  `SET_PANEL_MODE fail value: 0x14`. Identify the requesting feature and its
  expected value before changing the display HAL integration; no visible
  brightness, refresh-rate or panel malfunction was demonstrated by this audit.
- **Settings/audio Binder access:** six audit records deny `system_app` calls to
  `hal_audio_default` while Settings is active. The surrounding transaction-error
  callback logs need correlation with the Settings operation. A blanket policy
  allow is not justified by the denial alone.

## Errors that do not establish another feature regression

- **The earlier empty-volume-curves fault is absent:** the live audio policy now
  has 15 groups with 75 populated device-category curves. This verifies curve
  population, not a new listening or mute test. The
  [v23 audio record](audio-volume-curves-20260911.md) remains historical evidence.
- **Now Playing rearmed successfully:** two model reloads report successful QC
  ACD starts with new handles. The `already started` warning comes from
  `SoundModelStatTracker.onStart`, which maintains statistics; it is not a failed
  detector-start result. Its stale count can be cleaned up separately. No song
  identification was exercised by this audit.
- **Missing `libcsextimpl.so` is not newly actionable:** the
  [earlier factory comparison](camera-capture-diagnosis-20260907.md) found the
  optional extension absent in the factory extract too. There is no new camera
  capture failure tied to it. Camera downloadable modes were not exercised, so
  the older split-library problem is neither reconfirmed nor declared fixed.
- **Crash history:** this logcat snapshot contains no `FATAL EXCEPTION`, native
  `Fatal signal`, or `ANR in` record. Dropbox contains no new app crash/ANR entry
  in the current boot. Its earlier native tombstone identifies the `OmapiHold`
  diagnostic command from the eSIM investigation, not a spontaneously crashing
  system service. The QMI exit loop is independently confirmed by init.
- **Restart aftermath:** dead SystemUI Binder callbacks and ADB authentication
  socket errors overlap the earlier authorized SystemUI/ADB restart. They do not
  establish an additional spontaneous failure.
- **Other vendor fallbacks and SELinux denials:** unsupported codec enumeration,
  display writeback ROI fallback, and unsigned DSP service lookup denials do not
  by themselves prove an affected feature is broken. No broad policy relaxation
  is proposed.

The [notification limit source candidate](shade-notifications-20260912.md) remains
the previously prepared fix. This audit adds no ROM source patch and makes no
claim that any newly identified issue is fixed on the phone.
